09Common questions from Walnut Creek residents

Do I automatically have a claim if I was injured?

No. An injury alone does not establish legal responsibility. An attorney assesses the conduct involved, causation, the available proof, and applicable law.

What if I was partly responsible?

California uses comparative fault in many negligence cases. The precise effect depends on a fact-based allocation of responsibility and the legal claims involved.

Should I accept an early insurance offer?

Consider the completeness of medical information, known losses, reimbursement obligations, and the terms of any release before deciding. An offer is not the same as a verified valuation.

How soon should I request legal guidance?

Early advice can be helpful when evidence may disappear or deadlines may apply. California generally has a two-year personal-injury lawsuit limit, while government claims may require substantially earlier action.
